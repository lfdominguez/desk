title: The workspace
description: A card's editor view, where you read and edit its projects' files beside a second Claude that reads them with you, never writes, and answers the card's own Claude.
---

Every card has a workspace: its projects' files, an editor, and a second Claude, the workspace
Claude, that reads the code with you. The card's own Claude keeps doing the card's work; the
workspace is where you look over its shoulder, ask about what it did, and fix things yourself.

![DESK-3's workspace: the project tree on the left, login.go open with the change selected and marked in the gutter, the workspace Claude's answer on the right](../../../assets/shots/sess-workspace.png)

## Opening it

- In a card's panel or full window, click the **`</>`** icon in the header.
- Right-click the card and pick **Workspace** (<kbd>W</kbd> while the menu is open).
- From a [file change](/sessions/diffs/): the **`</>`** in a diff's header opens that file with the
  change selected (see [From a diff](#from-a-diff)).

<figure class="cut">

![The session header's icons, the workspace's first](../../../assets/shots/sess-workspace-open-btn.png)

<figcaption>The workspace icon in a session's header.</figcaption>
</figure>

<kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to the card's session, even from inside the editor. The chat icon at the
top right does the same.

The tree, the editor and the workspace Claude's chat sit side by side. Drag the line between two
of them to make the tree (160 to 480 dp) or the chat (300 to 720 dp) wider or narrower; desk keeps
the widths for every card's workspace (`[editor]` in
[settings.toml](/reference/desk-toml/#settingstoml)). In a narrow window the chat, then the tree,
give way so the editor keeps room.

## Projects

The tree on the left holds the card's projects. The first is always the card's own folder; it
can't be removed. The others are the folders the card's Claude works in: when it starts working
in a project root (a repository, or a folder with its own build) outside the card's folder, it
adds it with desk's `add_project` tool. desk takes the repository's root above the path it names
(the nearest folder with a `.git`, below your home), adds it at once and says so in a toast,
"Added lib to DESK-3's workspace", with **Undo** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> on the board).

<figure class="cut">

![The tree: api open, with internal, README.md and go.mod](../../../assets/shots/sess-workspace-tree.png)

<figcaption>The tree: folders first, each read when you open it. Files and tabs show their kind's icon, as VS Code's default (Seti) theme draws it.</figcaption>
</figure>

- A click on a folder opens or closes it; desk reads it as you open it, so a closed and reopened
  folder shows what's there now. `.git` and `node_modules` stay out.
- A click on a file opens it in a tab.
- The **×** on an added project's row takes it out of the workspace (not its files).

The projects are saved on the card (`projects` in the board file).

## The editor

Files open in tabs over the editor: line numbers, the code's highlighting (the same colors as the
chat's code), no wrapping, undo and redo (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>,
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>), <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd>
to indent selected lines. The foot shows the file's path and the caret's line and column.

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> saves the file in place, keeping its permissions. A tab with
  changes not saved shows **●** after its name; its **×** asks once before dropping them.
- Quitting desk with changes not saved keeps them (in `unsaved.json` beside the board, readable
  only by you): when you open that card's workspace again, its tabs come back with your text and
  the **●**. If a file changed on disk meanwhile, the bar below asks which to keep.
- When the file changes on disk (the card's Claude edited it, say) and you have no changes in it,
  the tab reloads by itself. desk looks when the card's Claude finishes an edit to that file, when
  you open the workspace and when you switch to the tab.
- If you do have changes in it, a bar asks: **Reload** takes the file as it is on disk, **Keep
  mine** keeps your text (saving it then writes over the other change).

Files over 4 MB, and files that aren't text, don't open.

## From a diff

Every [diff](/sessions/diffs/) in a card's conversation has a **`</>`** in its header: **Open in
workspace**.

<figure class="cut">

![A diff's header: its path, +5 −0, the workspace icon and the view switch](../../../assets/shots/sess-workspace-open-diff.png)

<figcaption>Open in workspace, in a diff's header.</figcaption>
</figure>

It opens the file, finds the change in it (by its new text: desk doesn't keep line numbers),
selects it, scrolls to it and marks it in the gutter. When the file no longer holds that text
(it changed since), the file opens at the top and the bar says so.

The bar over the editor offers **Ask about this change**, which puts the change, as a diff, in the
workspace Claude's message box for you to add your question, and **Done**, which clears the mark.

<figure class="cut">

![The bar: "The change to login.go from the chat, selected", Ask about this change, Done](../../../assets/shots/sess-workspace-bar.png)

<figcaption>The bar over a change opened from the chat.</figcaption>
</figure>

## The workspace Claude

The right column is the workspace Claude's conversation. It starts the first time you (or the
card's Claude) ask it something, in the card's folder with the other projects added, under the
organization's account. Its session is kept on the card and resumed the next time; a project
added while it runs restarts it (resumed) before its next answer.

<figure class="cut">

![The workspace Claude's column: a question, its Read, and its answer with a suggested diff](../../../assets/shots/sess-workspace-chat.png)

<figcaption>It reads the file, then suggests the change as a diff.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **It only reads.** Read, Glob, Grep and skills; Edit, Write and Bash are off, and anything it
  would ask to run is refused. It proposes changes as diffs or snippets, for you to make in the
  editor or pass on.
- **What you're looking at goes with your message**, by itself: the change you opened from the
  chat, else your selection with its line numbers, else the file open and the caret's line. The
  line under the box says which ("↵ sends with the selection in login.go").
- **It talks with the card's Claude.** The card's Claude can ask it something with desk's
  `ask_workspace` tool: the question shows in the workspace as **Card's Claude**, and the answer
  goes back into the card's conversation as **Workspace Claude** (to Claude, "From the workspace
  Claude:"), as the [infra Claude's](/servers/cards/) messages do. When you ask it to pass a
  suggestion on, it sends it the same way (`suggest_to_card`).

Its conversation is kept beside the card's (`chats/ws-<card>.jsonl`). Open tabs aren't kept: the
workspace opens with none when desk starts again.
