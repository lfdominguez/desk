In the [full window](/sessions/full-window/), a card's session can show as a **canvas**: its
process drawn as nodes and wires, the way Blender draws nodes. It draws what desk knows of the
card right now; nothing runs from it.

![DESK-3 run by "planner": the card, its ticket and the workspace Claude on the left, planner in the middle, Explore, test-writer and reviewer beside it with test-writer's shell, the pull request and its failed CI below, and Done on the right](../../../assets/shots/sess-canvas.png)

## What it draws

From left to right:

- **The card**: its title, labels and folder. It starts the session and gives it the card's
  context and its folder. Click it to [edit the card](/cards/editing/).
- **Its tickets**, each with its state. Click one to open it in the browser.
- **The workspace Claude**, once the card has one: read-only, it answers the session's questions.
  Click it to open the [workspace](/sessions/workspace/).
- **The session**: the card's Claude, or the agent the card runs as. It shows the card's status,
  turns, tokens and cost, and how full its context is. Click it to read its conversation.
- **Its subagents**, one node per kind of agent, each in its own color. A node shows the agent's
  model, its state (**Working**, **Waiting** when it's held, see
  [Held subagents](/sessions/subagents/#held-subagents), **Done · \<time\>**, or how it
  failed), its brief, its result once it's done, and its tools, tokens and time. An agent that ran
  several times shows "×N" and its latest run. Click it to open its conversation.
- **Shells** hang off whoever ran them: the session or a subagent. Click one to see its output.
- **Pull requests and commits**, with their state, and their **CI**: each job and how it went.
  Click a pull request to open it, its CI to open the failed run (or the first).
- **Other links** and the pages the session published.
- **Done**: the card's column. It turns cyan while the card waits in Review, green once you move
  it to Done.

**Thick white wires** with diamond sockets are the run order: the card starts the session, the
session hands work to its agents and shells, and finishes the card. **Colored wires** carry data,
colored by what they carry: the card's context (purple), its folder (green), changes (pink),
a pass or fail (cyan), words and links (grey). The session and its team sit in the **Session**
frame, what it delivers in the **Delivery** frame.

## Who is where

Each Claude's node carries an **avatar** in its header: its initials in its color, the session's,
each subagent's, the workspace Claude's. Over the node, a line says what it's on: the tool it runs,
what it's waiting on, or how it ended. A working avatar breathes, so you can tell at a glance who
is busy.

## Moving around

- **Drag** the canvas to pan it, or scroll.
- **Ctrl+scroll** (**⌘+scroll** on macOS) zooms around the pointer, from 40% to 250%.
- **Fit** shows the whole graph again. It opens fitted, and stays fitted as the graph grows until
  you pan or zoom.

Agents done for more than 20 minutes leave the canvas. A chip at the foot, "N earlier agents", brings
them back, and "Hide N earlier agents" sends them away again.

![The Explore agent's conversation in full: its prompt and its result](../../../assets/shots/sess-canvas-agent.png)

## Handoffs

**Handoffs** at the canvas's foot lists the team's handoffs beside it, in order, newest last: each
**brief** the session gave an agent, and each **result** an agent gave back. Each says when, and who
handed what to whom; the list ends with who the session still waits for. Click a handoff to open
that agent's conversation. **Hide handoffs** puts the list away.

## Canvas or Conversation

**Canvas | Conversation** in the header switches between the canvas and the session's own
conversation. A card that runs as an agent opens on its canvas, any other on its conversation;
desk keeps what you picked for each card until it quits. A question or a permission Claude asks
shows over the message box either way.

:::note
The canvas is only in the full window, and only once the session has started. In the side panel
the card's session shows as a conversation.
:::
