The full window shows a card's session in place of the board: the same conversation as the
[panel](/sessions/panel/), with room to read it. Its foot adds the context meter and the
account's plan limits.

![DESK-5's session in the full window: the sidebar on the left, the conversation in the middle, the usage strip above the message box](../../../assets/shots/sess-full.png)

## Opening it

There are three ways:

- In the card's panel, click **Open full window** (the first icon in its header).
- Right-click the card and pick **Open full window**.
- Click the card under **Agent Sessions** in the sidebar. While the card's
  [terminal](/sessions/terminal/) is open, this shows the terminal instead.

The toolbar then reads "DESK-5 · agent session". Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the **×** to go back to
the board.

## Agent Sessions

**Agent Sessions** in the sidebar lists every card that has a session, grouped by organization
when there is more than one. Each row has the card's status dot, its key and its title.
The row of the session you're looking at is lit.

<figure class="cut">

![The sidebar's Agent Sessions: DESK-6 under Acme, DESK-5 under Globex (lit), DESK-7 under Initech](../../../assets/shots/sess-full-sessions.png)

<figcaption>Agent Sessions, with DESK-5 open.</figcaption>
</figure>

- A row with a warning sign is waiting on you: Claude asked a question or wants permission.
- A row with a green terminal icon has its [terminal](/sessions/terminal/) open.
- Cards in Done leave the list. They're in the [Done archive](/board/archive/). Cards in Review
  stay until you move them to Done.

## Context and plan limits

The strip above the message box shows how full the session's context is, and how much of the
account's plan you have used.

<figure class="cut">

![The usage strip: Context 71k / 200k (36%), 5h 34% · resets 17:59, 7d 61% · resets Sat 15:49, 7d Opus 83% · resets Sat 15:49](../../../assets/shots/sess-full-usage.png)

<figcaption>Each figure has a small meter: green, orange from 70%, red from 90%.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Context** is the tokens in the session's context, out of the model's context window.
- **5h** is the plan's 5-hour session limit.
- **7d** is the week's limit, over every model.
- **7d \<model\>** is a model's own weekly limit, when the plan has one.

Each limit says when it resets: a time for today, or a weekday and time. See
[Tokens, cost and limits](/sessions/usage/) for where these figures come from.

## What else shows here

- The **canvas**: **Canvas | Conversation** in the header draws the card's process as nodes: the
  session, its subagents and shells, the workspace Claude, tickets, pull requests and CI. See
  [The canvas](/sessions/canvas/).
- The **rail** of subagents and background shells, opened by **Show details**. See
  [Subagents and shells](/sessions/subagents/).
- The **servers**: when the card's Claude handed work to its organization's infra Claude, the
  infra session shows beside the chat. **Chat only** in the header hides it, and
  **Split with \<org\> servers** brings it back. See [Cards and servers](/servers/cards/).
