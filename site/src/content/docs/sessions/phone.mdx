title: Your phone
description: A Telegram bot that brings cards that need you to your phone, where you allow or deny a command, pick an answer, or reply to a card's Claude.

When you're away from desk, a card that waits on you can wait a long time: a permission request,
a question, the end of its turn. With a Telegram bot, desk sends those to your phone, and you
answer there.

desk talks to Telegram by asking it for news (long polling), so nothing on your machine is opened
to the internet. It stops when desk quits.

## Setting it up

1. In Telegram, talk to **@BotFather**, send `/newbot` and follow it. It gives you the bot's
   token, `123456:ABC…`. Keep it to yourself: whoever has it can talk as the bot.
2. Give desk the token, from an environment variable or a command rather than as text when you
   can. In [Settings](/orgs/settings/#phone) → **Phone**, pick where **Bot token** comes from
   (**Environment variable**, **Command** or **In settings.toml**), or write it in
   `~/.config/desk/settings.toml`:

   ```toml
   [phone]
   token_env = "DESK_TELEGRAM_TOKEN"   # or token_cmd = "pass show desk/telegram", or token = "…"
   ```

3. Start desk, open your bot in Telegram and send it `/start` within 10 minutes. desk pairs that
   chat, says so in a toast and in the chat, and writes its `chat_id` into `settings.toml`. After
   that, the bot ignores every other chat. The Phone page then says **Paired with Telegram chat N**.

To pair another chat, click **Unpair** on the Phone page and **Save**, then send `/start` from that
chat within 10 minutes. A new token counts once desk starts again.

## What comes to the phone

The same moments as desk's desktop notifications: a card turns to your turn, blocked, failed or
ready for review. By default only while no desk window has the focus; **Also while desk has the
focus** on the Phone page (`always = true` under `[phone]`) sends them while you're at desk too.

| On the phone | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **A permission request** ("Wants to run Bash: …") | **Allow** or **Deny**. It's answered as desk's own Allow once and Deny are; a press on a request already answered says so |
| **A question** | its options as buttons when it's a single question with one answer; otherwise reply in your own words |
| **Its turn, a block, a failure** | the card's last message. Reply to it, and your reply goes to the card's Claude as your next message |

Each message starts with the card's key and title. A message that isn't a reply to one of the
bot's gets a reminder of how to answer.
