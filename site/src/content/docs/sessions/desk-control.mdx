---
title: Desk Control
description: Every card's session in a Telegram topic of its organization's group, where you follow it, answer its requests and write to its Claude.
---

Desk Control puts your cards in Telegram. Each organization you turn on has its own private
forum group, and every card with a conversation gets a **topic** there: you follow the session,
answer what it asks, and write to its Claude from your phone, while desk keeps running on your
machine. desk still does the work: Telegram is another window on it.

Set it up in [Settings → Telegram](/orgs/settings/#telegram): the bot, then **Log in** with your
account and **Set up / Repair** (or `/link Acme` in a group you made). Each organization stays off
until you pick what its group gets. A login that doesn't finish (canceled, or a wrong code or
2FA password) isn't kept, and one Telegram stops taking later is dropped with a note to log in again.

## A card's topic

The topic opens the first time the card has something to say. Its name is short, to read on a
phone: the card's state, its key and, when it tracks one, its ticket (`🟢 DESK-12 :: ABC-123`).
The title is in the pinned header.

| Name starts with | The card |
|---|---|
| 🟢 | Claude is working |
| 🟡 | your turn |
| 🔴 | blocked |
| ❌ | failed |
| 👀 | ready for review |
| ✅ | done |
| ⏸ | idle |

Its first message, pinned, is the card's header: its key and title, its column and state, its
links (pull requests, tickets) with their state as a list, and, in its footer, what the session has
cost, how many turns and how much of the context it uses. It changes in place as the card does.

A card that was already talking when you turned its organization on starts its topic with its last
message, not its whole history.

## What comes to the topic

That depends on what the organization's group gets:

| | Notify | Summary | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your messages written in desk | | ✓ | ✓ |
| Claude's replies | | ✓ | ✓ |
| Its tools (commands, edits), run together in a block folded under "🔧 N tools" | | | ✓ |
| Errors, and what other Claudes and leads tell it | | ✓ | ✓ |
| A turn that ends on you (your turn, blocked, failed, review) | ✓ | | |
| A permission request or a question, with buttons | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

Replies arrive as Claude finishes each one, in Telegram's rich text: headings, lists, tables, code
blocks. Who said what is in bold (**🧑 You** · _in desk_, **⚠️ Error**, **💬** another Claude or a
lead), and a divider sits between messages. Messages that come together go as one (up to 16,000
characters), cut where a message would get too long, without cutting a code block. A permission
request shows its shell command as a highlighted `sh` block. Anything that looks like a token or a private key is masked
(`‹redacted›`) before it leaves your machine, and so is what your own pattern matches
([**Also mask**](/orgs/settings/#telegram)). During your **Quiet hours** messages arrive without a
sound.

Telegram takes about one message every 3 seconds in a group, so a busy card catches up a few
seconds behind desk.

## Answering from the topic

- **A permission request** ("Wants to run …") comes with **Allow once**, **Deny** and **Allow
  every Bash** (the tool it asks for, for the rest of desk's run). A **question** with one answer
  comes with its options. Whichever side answers first counts, desk or the topic; the request's
  message then says *→ answered* and loses its buttons.
- **Anything you write** in the topic goes to the card's Claude as your message, as if typed in
  desk; desk reacts 👀 when it has it. It isn't sent back to the topic. A **picture** goes too,
  as an image of your message, its caption the text; **any other file** is saved beside the board
  and named in your message by its path, for Claude to read.
- **/stop** stops Claude's turn; **/help** says what the topic does. The group's command menu
  lists them, with the Infra topic's **/screen**.
- **/review** shows the card's [Review](/sessions/review/) when it works in its own worktree: the
  branch against its base, its commits, each changed file with its lines added and removed, and
  the whole diff as a `.diff` file. **Merge**, **Push** and **Open PR** each ask once more
  before they run, then say how it went.
- **/drawings** offers the card's [drawings](/drawings/overview/) and its organization's as buttons;
  the one you press comes as a picture.
- **/canvas** sends the card's [canvas](/sessions/canvas/) as a picture: its parts as boxes (the
  card, its Claudes, their checks, Done) with what each holds, joined as the canvas joins them,
  drawn in the drawings' hand.

## Drawing suggestions

When a Claude suggests a change to a drawing, the drawing as it would be comes as a picture with
the suggestion's note and **Approve**, **Rework** or **Dismiss**: in the card's topic when the card
has one, else in the bot's private chat (Inbox, with its topics on). Approve applies it as desk's
own Approve does, on the drawing open in desk or on its file. Rework asks what to change: your next
message in that chat goes back to the Claude who suggested it, as desk's Rework sends it, and its
next suggestion replaces this one (a command instead cancels). The infra Claude's suggestions have
no Rework, as in desk.

Only your own Telegram account, the one paired with the bot, is heard in the groups: anyone else's
messages and button presses are ignored. Text, pictures, files and voice notes reach Claude from a
card's topic, and from the General chat (an Infra topic takes text only). A voice note goes as its words, prefixed 🎙: Telegram's own
transcription makes them, asked as your logged-in account (it needs Telegram Premium, or the free
trials Telegram gives). It works in the bot's chat and the groups desk made; a group you linked by
hand with `/link` can't be named to your account, so there it says so.

## When the card moves

- To **Done**, or into the archive: the topic is deleted, so the list keeps only work under way.
  The conversation stays in desk; from Telegram, ask the desk Claude in the bot's chat about the
  card, or `/card DESK-12`.
- Back on the board: it gets a new topic, starting with its last message.
- Deleted: its topic goes too.

A topic an older desk closed opens again.

A card your organization's autopilot runs reports to the autopilot's card, not to a topic of its
own. While an organization is mirrored, its cards' news comes to its topics instead of to the
bot's private chat.

desk keeps which topic belongs to which card in `telegram.json`, beside the board.

## The Infra topic

An organization with [servers](/servers/overview/) gets one more topic in its group, **Infra**,
once an infra session is open. With your account logged in, desk pins **Infra** and the group's
**General** topic at the top of the topic list, so they don't sink below the cards (a bot can't
pin topics; a group you linked by hand isn't pinned). Its name follows the infra Claudes: 🔐 waiting for an approval,
🟢 working, ⏸ otherwise. Its pinned header lists each infra session (the organization's own, and a
card's) with its state and what each terminal runs right now ("running make deploy", "idle
prompt").

- **The organization's infra Claude's replies** come to the topic (not with Notify). A card's own
  infra session reports to the card instead: its results arrive in the card's topic as the peer's
  messages, and what it waits to run comes there too, with the same buttons, after any request of
  the card's own Claude. Only a card without a topic leaves its infra session's approvals to the
  Infra topic.
- **A command or change waiting for approval** comes with **Approve**, **Deny** and **Always**
  (allowed in this organization from then on, as the window's *always*). A file change or a
  servers file edit shows its diff and has no Always. Whichever side answers first counts; the
  message then says *→ answered*.
- **Anything you write** there goes to the organization's infra Claude as your message, opening
  its session if it has none (the window stays where it is); while it works, it's queued. Only
  text: a picture, file or voice note there is turned away.
- **/screen** sends a picture of a terminal as it is on screen, in desk's colors: the one running
  something, or the one you name (`/screen web1`). It's off until **Let /screen show a terminal**
  is on in [Settings → Telegram](/orgs/settings/#telegram): a terminal can show more than you mean
  to send. A picture can't be masked, so when something on the screen would be (a token, a
  password, your own **Also mask** pattern), it comes as text instead, masked.

## The General chat

Your private chat with the bot is desk's General chat. Cards of organizations that aren't mirrored
still bring their requests there (see [Your phone](/sessions/phone/)), and from it you work with the
whole board. The commands are in the chat's menu:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/board`, `/board Acme` | The board by column, each card with its state; Done only counted. |
| `/inbox` | The cards waiting on you: a request, your turn, a block, a failure, a review. |
| `/find login` | Cards on the board and in the archive that match, as desk's search matches. |
| `/card DESK-12` | One card: its header, notes and last reply. **Start it** when it hasn't started, **Stop its turn** while it works. Reply to the message to write to its Claude. |
| `/new @Acme fix the login #auth !high tomorrow` | A new card in To do, read as the [new card dialog](/board/cards/) reads it. Without an organization, buttons ask which. **Improve it** runs it through Improve with Claude, as the dialog's Improve (the tickets and pull requests it links read first), and the card takes the draft unless it started meanwhile; **Start it** starts it. |
| `/tickets`, `/alerts`, `/ci` | Your assigned tickets, Nagios alerts, and the CI that failed or is running; each takes an organization too. |
| `/digest`, `/digest week` | The [Digest](/board/digest/): today, `yesterday` or `week`. |
| `/company`, `/company Acme` | The [Company](/orgs/company/) in words: each organization's cards with a session by state (needs you, stuck, your turn, working, ready), the leads with briefs waiting, the last hand-offs and what it spent today. |
| `/drawings`, `/drawings Acme` | The drawings as buttons; the one you press comes as a picture. |
| `/review DESK-12` | A card's Review, as `/review` in its topic: its branch, the diff as a file, Merge, Push and Open PR, each asked twice. |
| `/canvas DESK-12` | A card's canvas as a picture, as `/canvas` in its topic. |
| `/widget` | The board as a live widget: each column's count and the cards waiting on you, kept up to date as the board changes. Tap a card and desk posts it here, as `/card` does. Widgets need a Telegram app that has them (Bot API 10.4). It keeps following the board after desk restarts; delete it in Telegram and desk stops. |
| `/usage` | Each Claude account's plan limits (as the session view's foot shows them), with the organizations using it. |
| `/always on`, `/always off` | Whether cards come here even while desk has the focus. |
| `/help` | What this chat does and its commands. |
| `/mirror Acme summary` | What an organization's group gets: `full`, `summary`, `notify` or `off`. |

A reply to a card's message can carry a picture or a file too: it goes to that card's Claude as
from its topic.

### Its own topics

With **topics** turned on for the bot in BotFather (*Bot Settings → Topics in private chats*), the
private chat gets four topics, each with a pinned message that follows the board:

| Topic | Its pinned message |
|---|---|
| 📋 Board | `/board`: every column and its cards. |
| 📥 Inbox | `/inbox`: what waits on you. Cards that need you come to this topic. |
| 📰 Digest | Today's [Digest](/board/digest/). A trigger's digest comes here. |
| ⚙️ Settings | Whether messages come while desk has the focus, whether `/screen` works, and each organization's mirror. |

A command or a question typed in a topic is answered in it. desk checks the switch when it
starts and when it's paired; without it, everything stays in the one chat.

### The desk Claude

Anything else you write goes to the **desk Claude**, a Claude with the whole board at hand: ask it
what's blocked, what Acme did this week, or to make a card or tell one something. It reads cards and
the digest, makes cards and messages them when you ask, and looks at a picture you send it. It
never edits files or runs commands, which stay the cards' work (it has desk's tools alone: no
Claude Code tool and none of your MCP servers); send a file as a reply to a card's
message instead. It runs in your own Claude profile, in desk's search folder, and answers once
it has finished. **/stop** stops its answer; **/forget** starts it afresh. Its conversation goes on
after desk restarts (its session is kept in `telegram-claude.txt`, beside the board) until you
**/forget** it.
