Most buttons in desk show their key on them, so you can also learn these as you go. These are
the default keys: you can [change them](#changing-shortcuts), and the buttons show yours.

Every key with a modifier has <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd> in it (<kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS counts
as <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>). <kbd>Shift</kbd> alone types: <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>2</kbd> is `@` on many
layouts.

:::note
Board keys work on the board only: not while you type in a field, not in a dialog, and not in the full-window session view. A card's terminal takes every key, <kbd>Esc</kbd> included, for Claude Code. The infra workspace has its own keys, on <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>.
:::

## Anywhere

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Backs out of the innermost thing, one per press: the palette, the unlock dialog, the server picker, the settings, the card editor (it saves), the new-card dialog, the card menu, the snooze picker; then clears the search, leaves an answer field, goes back from a subagent or shell to the conversation, closes the rail, the session panel, the full-window session, and last the selection. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> | Opens the command palette. Works over the message box too, but not over a dialog. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> | Opens the session in view (else the selected card's) [in its own window](/sessions/full-window/#in-its-own-window); in that window, puts it back. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>=</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd> | Make the text of the terminals (a card's Claude Code terminal and the servers' panes) and of the session panel bigger or smaller, 10% a press, from 50% to 300%. Also over a field and inside a terminal: the program doesn't get them. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> | The text back to 100%. |

## Changing shortcuts

**Settings → Keyboard shortcuts** (or **Keyboard shortcuts** in the command palette) lists every
shortcut by where it works, with its keys as they are now.

![The Keyboard shortcuts page in Settings: Reset all on top, then the Anywhere and Board shortcuts, each with its keys and Change; Esc and the arrows marked fixed](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-keys.png)

- **Change**, or a click on the row's keys, waits for the next key you press, with its modifiers, and makes it the shortcut's.
  <kbd>Esc</kbd> or **Cancel** cancels. A key that breaks the rule is refused, with why under the row: a modifier
  needs <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd>, and a key that types (a letter, a digit, a symbol,
  <kbd>Space</kbd>) works alone only for shortcuts that never fire while you type (the board's
  <kbd>E</kbd>, a drawing's tools).

  ![New card's row waiting for its new key: "Press a key…" where its keys were, and Cancel in place of Change](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-keys-change-row.png)
- A key another shortcut in the same place already uses is named under the row, and Save refuses
  until one of them changes.

  ![New card changed to Ctrl+Z, with "also runs Undo an archive or a delete, while its toast shows — change one" under it](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-keys-clash-row.png)

- **Reset** puts one back; **Reset all** every one. Rows marked *fixed* (Enter, Esc, arrows, the
  answers of a permission or approval box) can't change.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> saves, with the other settings' pages. Buttons, and the workspace
  editor's tabs already open, take the new keys at once.

What you change is kept in `settings.toml` (`~/.config/desk/`), under `[keys]`, one line per
shortcut you changed:

```toml
[keys]
"board.new" = "Alt+B"
"text.newline" = "Ctrl+Enter"
```

A line desk can't use (a name it doesn't know, a key that breaks the rule) is skipped at start,
and a message says which. A shortcut is one key with its modifiers; sequences (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>
then a key) aren't supported.
