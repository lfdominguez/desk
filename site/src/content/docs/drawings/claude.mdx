Every Claude desk runs can read the organization's drawings, and draw in them when you ask.
Each drawing also has a Claude of its own, in a chat beside the canvas.

![Login flow with its Claude: asked for a cache, it drew Cache under Sessions, marked as its own, and suggested an Audit log, which floats as a ghost with Approve, Rework and Dismiss](../../../assets/shots/drawings-claude.png)

## The drawing's Claude

The chat button at the right of the drawing's header shows or hides it. Ask it to draw or
change something: what you have selected goes with your message, so "make these blue" works.
<kbd>Enter</kbd> sends, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> is a new line.

It runs with the organization's account, in its folder, so it can read the organization's files
to draw from them. A card's drawing's Claude runs in the card's folder instead, with the
projects of the card's [workspace](/sessions/workspace/), and reads only there unless you ask it
to look elsewhere. It draws only through the drawing tools: it never changes a file or runs a
command. A card's drawing's Claude knows which card the drawing belongs to. It is not the card's
session, though; the card's own Claude can draw too.

A label wraps to its shape's width, as in Excalidraw, and the shape grows taller to hold it.
Arrows and lines a Claude draws with bends get sharp corners, unless it asks for round ones.

Its conversation is kept with the drawing and picks up where it left off, across restarts.

## Claude's edits

When a Claude draws in a drawing you have open, you see it happen. What it drew is outlined in
its color, with its name under it, until you next press on the canvas. Each of its edits is one
step: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> takes it back. A drawing that isn't open is changed in its
file.

Claude draws only when you ask it to. What it thinks would help but you didn't ask for comes as a
suggestion.

## Suggestions

A suggestion floats over the drawing in its Claude's color: what it would add, see-through;
what it would change, as it would look; what it would delete, crossed out. Its card at the bottom
right says who suggested it and why:

- **Approve** makes the change, as one step you can undo.
- **Rework** opens a box for your comment, which goes back to that Claude. Its next suggestion
  replaces this one. The infra Claude's suggestions have no Rework: there is no conversation to
  send it to.
- **Dismiss** drops it.

Each Claude has at most one suggestion waiting on a drawing: a new one replaces its last.
Suggestions are kept beside the drawing, never in its file, so they don't reach excalidraw.com.

## The drawing tools

Every Claude gets these tools from desk:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `drawings` | Lists the drawings it can read: the organization's and, for a card's Claude, the card's own. |
| `drawing_read` | A drawing as a picture, and its elements one a line: id, type, place and size, colors, label, frame, groups, and what an arrow joins. Your unsaved changes are in it. |
| `drawing_edit` | Adds, changes and deletes elements, as one step you can undo. A name not taken makes a new drawing (in the card's own drawings, for a card's Claude). |
| `drawing_suggest` | Proposes a change instead, with a note, for you to approve. |

Who gets which:

| Claude | Tools |
|---|---|
| A drawing's own | all four; its own drawing unless it names another |
| A card's session | all four |
| A card's [workspace](/sessions/workspace/) Claude | `drawings`, `drawing_read` and `drawing_suggest`: it only reads |
| The [infra Claude](/servers/infra-claude/) | all four |
