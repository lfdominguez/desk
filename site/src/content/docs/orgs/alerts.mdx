Under each organization with one or more `[[nagios]]` accounts, the sidebar has **Alerts**: the
hosts that are down or unreachable and the services in warning or critical, errors first.

![Globex's alerts: web-02 HTTP critical under Errors, db-01 Disk /var warning under Warnings, each with Snooze and Investigate](../../../assets/shots/orgs-alerts.png)

## Connecting Nagios

Add a Nagios account to the organization, in [Settings](/orgs/settings/) or in its
`desk.toml` (see [Accounts](/orgs/accounts/)):

```toml
[[nagios]]                      # as many as the organization has
name = "prod"                   # what its alerts say they come from (default: its host)
url = "https://mon.globex.example.com/nagios"   # its web root, where cgi-bin/ is
user = "me"                     # the CGIs' login; the password is the token
vault = "globex"
item = "Nagios prod"
```

desk reads Nagios Core's JSON CGI (`statusjson.cgi`, which Nagios XI serves too) with basic or
digest auth, and falls back to a Nagios 3's `status.cgi` pages. The alerts refresh with the
links, every 3 minutes, and when you **Reload**.

## Reading the list

<figure class="cut">

![The alert web-02 · HTTP: CRITICAL, 7 min. ago, from prod, its output "HTTP CRITICAL: HTTP/1.1 502 Bad Gateway", and the buttons Snooze and Investigate](../../../assets/shots/orgs-alerts-row.png)

<figcaption>One alert: its state, host and service, since when, which Nagios, and what the check said.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Errors** (critical, down, unreachable) come first, then **Warnings**, then **Snoozed**.
- An acknowledged alert is dimmed and says *acknowledged*.
- Clicking an alert opens it in Nagios.
- The search box filters by monitor, host, service and output.

The header counts them: *1 error · 1 warning*, plus how many are acknowledged and snoozed. The
sidebar's **Alerts** row counts the ones nobody acknowledged or snoozed, in the color of the
worst of them (red for an error, orange for a warning).

## Investigate

<figure class="cut">

![The buttons Snooze and Investigate; Investigate in blue](../../../assets/shots/orgs-alerts-actions.png)

<figcaption>Investigate is blue on an error nobody acknowledged or snoozed.</figcaption>
</figure>

When the organization has servers (an [infra workspace](/servers/overview/)), each alert has
**Investigate**. It makes the alert a card, titled *Investigate CRITICAL web-02 / HTTP*, in
**In progress**, with the alert's output and link in its notes. It opens the card's session and
asks its Claude to look into it: the monitoring and metrics, and the servers through the
organization's infra Claude, with read-only commands. Claude finds the likely cause and proposes a
fix; nothing changes on the servers without your approval.

![The session of the new card DESK-9, Investigate CRITICAL web-02 / HTTP, with the message asking Claude to investigate](../../../assets/shots/orgs-investigate.png)

An alert that already has an open card (not in Done; Review counts as open) opens that card instead of making another.

When the investigation ends with the alert left as it is on purpose (a container stopped by hand,
an expected state), Claude says so, and the card offers to snooze the alert above its message
box, with the same choices as below.

## Snooze

**Snooze** hides an alert, and takes it out of the count, until a time you pick. Use it for an
alert you've looked into and are leaving as it is. desk keeps snoozes next to the board
(`snoozed.json`); Nagios isn't told.

<figure class="cut">

![The Snooze until… popover for web-02 / HTTP: 1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day and 1 week with when each ends and the keys 1 to 4, a field "Or type a time", Cancel and Snooze 1 hour](../../../assets/shots/orgs-alerts-snooze-picker.png)

<figcaption>Each duration says when it ends.</figcaption>
</figure>

- Click **1 hour**, **4 hours**, **1 day** or **1 week**, or press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd>.
- Or type your own time under *Or type a time*: `2h30m`, `3d`, `1w`. desk reads it as you type and
  says until when; <kbd>Enter</kbd> or **Snooze** takes it.
- **Cancel** closes it.

A snoozed alert moves to **Snoozed**, says until when, and gets **Unsnooze** to bring it back
before then.

<figure class="cut">

![db-01 Disk /var under Warnings; web-02 HTTP under Snoozed, dimmed, saying until when it is snoozed, with Unsnooze](../../../assets/shots/orgs-alerts-snoozed-list.png)

<figcaption>web-02 snoozed for 4 hours: out of the count, at the bottom.</figcaption>
</figure>

A card's Claude can read the same alerts with the [card tools](/orgs/card-tools/).
