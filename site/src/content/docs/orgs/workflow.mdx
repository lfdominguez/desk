---
title: Ticket workflow
description: Teach desk how an organization's tickets move, as its people use them - what each status means, who moves tickets where - so cards, tickets and the card's Claude follow it.
---

By default desk moves a card's ticket by the column's status category (see
[Tickets follow their card](/cards/links/#tickets-follow-their-card)), and moves back any ticket
that drifted. That works until an organization's statuses mean something of their own: its
**Test** may mean *deployed to the testing environment*, not *someone is testing it*, and only
QA moves a ticket to **Done**. The **ticket workflow** tells desk how that organization works.

## Learning it

In [Settings](/orgs/settings/), open the organization in the nav and pick **Ticket workflow**.

1. In **Instructions**, write how its tickets move, or where to read it: *Read ./docs/process.
   "Test" means deployed to the testing environment; only QA moves a ticket to Done.* Paths start
   at the organization's folder.
2. **Learn** has Haiku read it, and every file it points at (read-only), next to the statuses of
   the organization's Jira projects and the states (with their types) of its Linear teams, which
   desk fetches. It runs on the provider set for **Learn
   workflow** in [Models](/orgs/models/). **Stop** ends it.
3. What it understood fills the fields below, for you to check and change:
   - **What Haiku understood**: its summary, and what was unclear or missing.
   - **Card in \<column\>**: the status its ticket goes to when a card moves there. Empty: the
     status category's guess, as without a workflow. An account's own `[jira.status]` or `[linear.status]` still wins.
   - **Statuses others move tickets to**: one `Status → Column` per line. When someone puts a
     ticket in one of them, the card follows to that column.
   - **Rules for the card's Claude**: what each status means here, when Claude moves a ticket
     itself, and what it must never do.
4. **Save**. Nothing applies before.

Save refuses a line of the statuses others move to that doesn't name a column of the board.

## What changes

- **Every card's Claude** of the organization is told the rules, and gets a `ticket_status` tool to
  move a ticket of its card by the status's name (a Linear issue: a state of its team) when the rules say so (after it deploys to
  testing, say). A status it can't reach comes back with the ones it can. Only your own tickets
  move, never through an account with `sync = false`, nor out of a status its `ignore` leaves to
  others.
- **The card follows its ticket.** When someone moves a ticket to a status the workflow gives a
  column (the statuses others move to, or a column's status read backwards), its card moves
  there, with a toast: *ABC-131 is Test: DESK-12 follows to Review*. The same when the card's
  Claude moves it. When several columns share that status, a card already in one of them stays;
  otherwise it goes to the first of them on the board. A GitHub issue closed takes its card to
  Done, and reopened, out of Done to In progress.
- **No ticket is moved back.** The check at start and every 3 minutes leaves a ticket someone
  moved where it is: either its card follows it, or both stay.
- **Moving a card yourself** still moves its ticket, to the column's status. When it can't (the
  ticket is someone else's, or the move isn't allowed there), the card stays where you put it:
  a card follows only a status that changed since the last check, and the first check of a
  ticket only notes where it is.

## In desk.toml

```toml
[workflow]
instructions = "Read ./docs/process"
summary = "Test means deployed to the testing environment; QA closes tickets."
rules = "Move a ticket to Test after you deploy it to the testing environment. Never move one to Done."
learned = "2026-11-17"

[workflow.status]          # a column → its ticket's status
"In progress" = "In Progress"
Review = "Code Review"

[workflow.follow]          # a status others move to → the card's column
Test = "Review"
Deployed = "Done"
```

The statuses are names: a Jira status, or a Linear state of the issue's team. They apply to an
organization's Jira and Linear tickets alike.
