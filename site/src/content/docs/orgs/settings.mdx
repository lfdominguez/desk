The Settings dialog edits two kinds of files: desk's own settings (`~/.config/desk/settings.toml`:
who you are, the folders it searches, the languages, Telegram, diagnostics, the shortcuts) and
each organization's `desk.toml` (its accounts and everything else it sets).

![The Settings dialog on Acme's Accounts page: the nav on the left, Acme's GitHub and Jira accounts, the buttons that add an account, and Save at the bottom](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-org.png)

## Opening it

- **Settings**, the gear in the header of the sidebar's Organizations section. It opens on the organization
  you're looking at: the view's, else the selected card's, else the last one you used.
- The [command palette](/board/palette/) has **Settings**, **Open \<org\> settings** for each
  organization, and **Search folders**.

## The nav

<figure class="cut">

![The settings' nav: the Search settings box, then the desk group with General, Search folders, Telegram, Keyboard shortcuts and Diagnostics](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-general-nav.png)

<figcaption>The search box, then desk's own pages.</figcaption>
</figure>

The left column has two groups under a search box:

- **desk**: [General](#general), [Search folders](#search-folders), [Telegram](#telegram),
  **Keyboard shortcuts** (see [Changing shortcuts](/reference/keys/#changing-shortcuts)) and
  [Diagnostics](#diagnostics).
- **Organizations · N**: every organization, a nested one under its outer one. Click one to open
  it: its parts show under it and its **Accounts** page opens. Click the open one again to fold it.

Each organization's dot says how its `desk.toml` reads:

| Dot | Means |
|---|---|
| green | it reads and has accounts |
| orange | it doesn't read (a TOML error); the row says *can't read* |
| grey | no accounts yet; the row says *no accounts* |

A page you changed says *edited*; a nested organization says *in \<outer\>*. The bottom of the nav
shows the desk version that runs.

An organization's page is titled with the organization and the part (**Acme · Triggers**); under
it, what the part is for and the path of its `desk.toml`.

### Searching

**Search settings** matches every word you type against each page's title and the words of its
fields, across every organization. Typing `worktree` lists **Acme › Git & review**, **Globex ›
Git & review** and so on; click one to open it. With no match the nav says *Nothing matches.*

![The nav searching "worktree": Acme › Git & review, Globex › Git & review and Initech › Git & review](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-search-nav.png)

<figcaption>One search, every organization's page that matches.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Saving

The bar at the bottom counts the unsaved changes and says which file the page saves. You can move
between pages, and between organizations: each keeps its edits until you save or discard.

- **Save** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) writes every page you changed, the one shown first, and
  closes the dialog. A page that can't be saved is shown with the reason in the bar.
- **Discard** drops every page's changes.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> or **×** with unsaved changes first turns the bar orange and asks; <kbd>Esc</kbd>
  again closes without them.

<figure class="cut">

![The settings' bar: "No changes · saves ~/Work/Acme/.desk/desk.toml; comments in it are not kept", and Save with Ctrl+S](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-org-bar.png)

<figcaption>The bar names the file the page writes.</figcaption>
</figure>

:::caution
Saving rewrites the `desk.toml` from what it read. Keys the dialog doesn't show are kept, but
comments in the file are not. If you keep a commented `desk.toml` by hand, edit it by hand.
:::

## General

![The General page: Who am I?, the two language fields, the text size and Cards use my MCP servers](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-general.png)

### Who am I

<figure class="cut">

![The Who am I? field, empty, with its example, and "Told to the Claudes desk starts for your work" under it](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-general-me.png)

<figcaption>Who you are, for every organization unless one replaces it.</figcaption>
</figure>

**Who am I?** is you, in your own words: your role, what you know, how you like answers and work
done ("I'm the backend lead: Go and Postgres. Keep answers short, ask before touching
production."). Every Claude desk starts is told it (card sessions and their terminal, the
workspace's, the leads, drawings, the infra Claude and Improve with Claude), to focus its answers
and choices on you. It never overrides a repository's rules or what you ask in a message.

An organization has its own in its [About me](#about-me) page.

### Languages

<figure class="cut">

![The fields "Cards and tickets in" and "Claude speaks with you in", both English](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-general-langs.png)

<figcaption>The languages new cards start with. Each card can change its own.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Cards and tickets in**: what Improve with Claude, tickets and what Claude writes on the card
  (status reasons, ticket titles, commit messages unless the repository says otherwise) are
  written in.
- **Claude speaks with you in**: the language of the card's conversation.

Both are English until you change them.

### Text size

<figure class="cut">

![Text size: − 100% +, and "Terminals and the session panel · Ctrl+= bigger, Ctrl+- smaller, Ctrl+0 back to 100%"](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-general-zoom.png)

<figcaption>The text size, at 100%.</figcaption>
</figure>

How big the text of the terminals (a card's Claude Code terminal and the servers' panes) and of the
session panel is, from 50% to 300%. **−** and **+** change it by 10% and it counts at once, without
**Save**. Anywhere in desk, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>=</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd> and
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> (back to 100%) do the same. A terminal's columns and rows follow the
text, so a bigger text gives its program fewer of them.

### Cards use my MCP servers

Off unless you turn it on (`user_mcp` in `settings.toml`). Off, a card's Claude (its session and
its terminal) has desk's own tools and none of the MCP servers you set up for Claude Code: each
server's tools are sent with every turn, whether the card uses them or not. Turn it on when your
cards rely on one of yours. Sessions started after **Save** take the change; a running one keeps
what it started with.

## Search folders

![The Search folders page: the search folder ~/Work with depth 2, and the preview of the organizations it finds](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-folders.png)

The folders desk searches for organizations, top to bottom: when two give the same name, the
higher one wins. Each row has:

<figure class="cut">

![A search folder row: the path ~/Work, "finds 5 · Acme, Globex, Initech, scratch, Billing", depth 2 with − and +, and the two switches](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-folders-folder.png)

<figcaption>One search folder. Under the path, what it finds.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Folder**: a full path, or one from `~`.
- **Depth**: − and + from 0 to 6. Level 1 is the folder's subfolders; deeper levels count only
  folders with a `desk.toml`.
- **Only with desk.toml**: subfolders without a `desk.toml` aren't organizations. Typing one of
  them in a card's organization field says it has no `desk.toml`.
- **Folder is an org**: the folder itself is an organization when it has a `desk.toml`.
- **×** removes the folder.

**+ Add folder** adds a row (depth 1, *Only with desk.toml* on).

The **Preview** under the rows scans the folders as you edit them and shows the organizations
they give: a new one says where it comes from, a nested one shows its path. With *Only with
desk.toml* on, the subfolders it leaves out are listed as skipped (**Show all** past six).

<figure class="cut">

![With "Only with desk.toml" on: the preview shows Acme, Globex, Initech and Billing · Acme/billing, and "Skipped, no desk.toml: scratch"](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-only-preview.png)

<figcaption>Turning on Only with desk.toml: scratch has none, so it's skipped.</figcaption>
</figure>

Save refuses a depth that isn't 0 to 6, a path that isn't a folder, and an empty list (desk needs
one folder to find organizations). Saving the folders also reloads the organizations.

## Telegram

Desk Control: the Telegram bot that brings cards that need you to your phone (see
[Your phone](/sessions/phone/)), and a forum group per organization. It's settings.toml's
`[telegram]` (an older `[phone]` moves there by itself). The page has three parts.

### Bot

<figure class="cut">

![The Telegram page's Bot part: "Not paired: send /start to your bot within 10 minutes of starting desk. That chat becomes the General chat.", the bot token from Environment variable DESK_TELEGRAM_TOKEN, and the switches Also while desk has the focus and Let /screen show a terminal, then the empty fields Quiet hours and Also mask](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-telegram-bot.png)

<figcaption>A bot token, not paired yet.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Bot token**: where the bot's token comes from: **None**, **Environment variable**,
  **Command**, or **In settings.toml**.
- The pairing: **Paired with Telegram chat N: the General chat** with **Unpair** (on Save, a
  `/start` within 10 minutes pairs a chat), or *Not paired: send /start to your bot within 10
  minutes of starting desk.*
- **Also while desk has the focus**: off, the phone hears from desk only while no desk window is in
  front.
- **Let /screen show a terminal**: off by default. On, `/screen` in an organization's
  [Infra topic](/sessions/desk-control/#the-infra-topic) sends a picture of a server terminal as it
  is on screen (`screen = true` under `[telegram]`). A picture can't have tokens masked as text
  does: turn it on knowing that.
- **Quiet hours**: two times like `22:00-07:00` (`quiet` under `[telegram]`). Between them, what
  desk sends still arrives, without a sound; past midnight works. Empty: never quiet.
- **Also mask**: a regular expression (Go's syntax, `ACME-\d+|hunter2`; `redact` under
  `[telegram]`) masked like a token in everything desk sends to Telegram, besides the secrets it
  already knows. Save refuses one that doesn't read.

### Your account, for setup

<figure class="cut">

![The account part: Not logged in with Log in, App api_id 1234567, App api_hash from Environment variable DESK_TELEGRAM_API_HASH, Your phone number, and Folder](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-telegram-account.png)

<figcaption>Saved, not logged in yet.</figcaption>
</figure>

A bot can't make groups or folders; your own Telegram account can. desk logs in as you only to set
Desk Control up:

- **App api_id** and **App api_hash**: an app of yours, from
  [my.telegram.org](https://my.telegram.org) → **API development tools**. The hash comes from an
  environment variable, a command or settings.toml.
- **Your phone number**, as Telegram knows it.
- **Folder**: the chat folder's name, **Desk Control** unless you name it (12 characters at most:
  Telegram's limit).

**Save**, then **Log in**: the code Telegram sends you and, when your account has one, its 2FA
password are asked right there, and go from this window to Telegram only. The session is kept in
desk's state folder (`~/.local/state/desk/telegram.session`), readable by you alone; **Log out**
ends it at Telegram and removes it. A login that doesn't finish (canceled, a wrong code or
password) isn't kept, and a session Telegram stops taking (ended from another device) is dropped
the next time desk uses it, with a note to log in again.

### Organizations

<figure class="cut">

![The Organizations part: Acme on Full, Globex on Notify, Initech Off, each "no group yet", the note on what each means, and Set up / Repair](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-telegram-more-orgs.png)

<figcaption>Acme mirrored fully, Globex only what needs you.</figcaption>
</figure>

What each organization's group gets: **Off**, **Notify** (the cards that need you), **Summary**
(also Claude's replies) or **Full** (the whole session, its tools and diffs). Telegram chats aren't
end-to-end encrypted, so what goes there is on Telegram's servers: each organization is off until
you turn it on.

**Set up / Repair** makes what's missing: a private forum group for each organization that isn't
off, with the bot as an admin that manages topics, then the folder holding the bot's chat and
those groups. Running it again only adds what's new. When Telegram asks desk to slow down (making
several groups in a row), desk waits the seconds it names, up to two minutes, and goes on. A step
Telegram refuses stops it with *Set up stopped:* and why; the groups made before are kept, and the
next press picks up from there. Next to each organization, *group ready* or
*no group yet*.

Without logging in, make a group yourself: turn on **Topics** in its settings, add the bot as an
admin, and send `/link Acme` in it (only from your own account, the one paired with the bot). The
organization shows *linked by hand*; add that group to the folder yourself.

## Diagnostics

<figure class="cut">

![The Diagnostics page: the Sentry DSN field and its switches: Errors, Freezes, Breadcrumbs, Logs, Metrics, AI agent monitoring](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-diag-page.png)

<figcaption>Empty, nothing is sent.</figcaption>
</figure>

settings.toml's `[sentry]`: the **Sentry DSN**. With one, desk reports its own crashes to that
Sentry project: what failed, where, and which version. The switches under it, all off until you
turn them on, add **Errors** (failures shown only as a toast), **Freezes** (a window not
responding, with its stacks), **Breadcrumbs** (the actions before an error or crash), **Logs**,
**Metrics** (turn time, tokens and cost by model) and **AI agent monitoring** (each card turn with
its tools). No card's words, prompt or reply is sent. It counts once desk starts again. See
[Report to Sentry](/reference/troubleshooting/#report-to-sentry).

## An organization's parts

### Accounts

Each account is a card: its kind's tile (GH, JI, LN, BW…), its name and kind, and a line with
where it points and where its token comes from. **Edit** opens it; one is open at a time.

<figure class="cut">

![Acme's Jira account open: Name, URL, Email and Projects fields, the API token's source set to Environment variable with DESK_DEMO_TOKEN, and Remove](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-account.png)

<figcaption>An account open: its fields two by two, where its token comes from, and Remove.</figcaption>
</figure>

A card says **New** or **Edited** until you save. The buttons under the cards add an account of
each kind (**+ Linear** and **+ Model provider** among them):

<figure class="cut">

![The buttons + Vault, + GitHub, + Jira, + Linear, + Bitbucket, + GitLab, + Nagios and + Model provider](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-org-add.png)

<figcaption>Add a vault or an account.</figcaption>
</figure>

What each kind's fields mean, and where tokens come from, is in [Accounts](/orgs/accounts/),
[Vaults](/orgs/vaults/) and [Model providers](/orgs/models/). Jira and Linear cards also have a
switch that logs a card's work time on its ticket at Done (`worklog`), the same one as in
[Git & review](#git--review).

Save refuses a vault without a name, two vaults with the same name, and a token from a vault the
organization doesn't have; a model provider without a name or a base URL, and two with the same
name.

### Ticket workflow

How the organization's tickets move, as its people use them, with **Learn** to have Haiku read
it: see [Ticket workflow](/orgs/workflow/).

### Git & review

<figure class="cut">

![Git & review: Each card on its own worktree, on; then Work time on tickets with a switch for Jira · acme](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-git-page.png)

<figcaption>Worktrees on, and Acme's Jira account logging work time.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Each card on its own worktree** (`[git] worktrees`): a new card in a git repository gets its
  own branch and worktree, and a Review that merges, pushes or opens a pull request. See
  [Review & ship](/sessions/review/).
- **Work time on tickets**: a switch per Jira or Linear account (its `worklog`). When a card goes
  to Done, the time its Claude worked goes on its ticket: a worklog in Jira, a comment in Linear.
  See [Digest](/board/digest/#work-time).

### Triggers

<figure class="cut">

![Triggers: "New tickets" (ticket · start its claude) and "Fix red CI" (ci_failed) folded, and "Weekly audit" open: On schedule, At 09:00, Days mon, Card title Dependency audit, Column Backlog](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-triggers-page.png)

<figcaption>Three triggers, one open.</figcaption>
</figure>

A card per `[[trigger]]`; **+ Trigger** adds one. Its fields: **Name**, **On** (`ticket`,
`ci_failed`, `alert`, `schedule` or `digest`), **Project**, **Match**, **At**, **Days**, **After
minutes**, **Column**, **Card title**, **Card notes**, and the switches **Start its Claude**,
**Alert: warnings too** and **Off**. Each field's hint says which kinds use it. Save refuses a
trigger without a name, and **After minutes** that isn't a whole number. What each kind does is in
[Triggers](/orgs/triggers/).

### Models

Which of desk's Claudes run on your Claude subscription and which on one of the organization's
model providers: see [Which Claude runs where](/orgs/models/#which-claude-runs-where). Save
refuses a Claude on a provider the organization doesn't have.

### Company

<figure class="cut">

![Company: Per card, dollars 5 and Per day, dollars 40](../../../assets/shots/orgs-settings-company-budgets.png)

<figcaption>Budgets of 5 dollars a card and 40 a day.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Per card, dollars** and **Per day, dollars** (`[company] card_budget`, `day_budget`): a
  card's session pauses at the end of a turn once it has spent its budget since your last
  message, or the organization its budget for the day. Empty: no limit. See
  [Budgets](/orgs/company/#budgets).
- **Company roles**: the model each of desk's roles runs on. See
  [The roles' models](/orgs/models/#the-roles-models).

### Servers

The **Show the server's login message** switch (`clear_login = false`: the banner and message of
the day stay above a pane's first prompt, see [Command blocks](/servers/panes/#command-blocks)),
the organization's servers file and **Import from Ásbrú** ([Import from Ásbrú](/servers/asbru/)).

### About me

**Who am I here**, for who you are in this organization, and **Extend from global**: on (the
default), its Claudes get the global [Who am I](#who-am-i), then the organization's; off, only the
organization's. It is the organization's `[me]` in its desk.toml.

### When desk.toml doesn't read

Its pages say why in the bar ("… — fix it by hand") and have no Save: fix the file by hand, then
open the settings again.
