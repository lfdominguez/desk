---
title: Triggers
description: Rules in an organization's desk.toml that make cards and tell Claudes by themselves, from new tickets, failed CI, monitoring alerts and the time of day.
---

Tickets, CI and alerts each become a card when you click them. A trigger does that click for you:
a rule in the organization's `desk.toml` that desk follows on its own.

```toml
[[trigger]]
name = "new tickets"
on = "ticket"
project = "ABC"
start = true

[[trigger]]
name = "fix red CI"
on = "ci_failed"

[[trigger]]
name = "database down"
on = "alert"
match = "db"
after = 10

[[trigger]]
name = "weekly audit"
on = "schedule"
at = "09:00"
days = ["mon"]
title = "Dependency audit"
notes = "Check every repository's dependencies for known vulnerabilities and outdated majors."
```

## What each watches

| `on` | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `ticket` | a ticket assigned to you shows up in the organization's [Tickets](/orgs/tickets/) with no card yet (of `project`, with `match` in its title, when given) | a card for it in To do, titled with its key and title, the ticket linked; `start` starts its Claude |
| `ci_failed` | a pull request or commit linked on a card that isn't in Done fails a CI run (what the [CI](/orgs/ci/) view shows) | tells the card's Claude which run failed, to read it with `ci` and `ci_log` and fix it |
| `alert` | a monitoring [alert](/orgs/alerts/) nobody acknowledged or snoozed: an error (and warnings with `warnings`), that has lasted `after` minutes, its host or service matching `match` | an investigation card in progress, as **Investigate** makes, its Claude started on it |
| `schedule` | the time `at` (local), on `days` (every day without them) | a card with `title` and `notes`; `start` starts its Claude |

Every card a trigger makes says so at the end of its notes: "(Made by the trigger weekly audit.)".
A toast says which trigger made which card. `column` puts its cards in another column.

## When they run

Triggers run after each check desk makes of links, tickets, CI and alerts: when desk starts, and
every 3 minutes after. A schedule therefore runs within 3 minutes of its time, once that day.

What each trigger has handled (tickets, CI runs, alerts by when they started, a schedule's last
day) is kept in the organization's `.desk/triggers.json`, so nothing is done twice, not even across
restarts. A trigger's first run only notes what is already there: turning one on doesn't make a
card for every ticket you already have, and a schedule added after its time runs from the next
day. `off = true` pauses a trigger and keeps what it handled.

An `on` desk doesn't know says so in a toast.
