The sidebar's **Digest**, under All cards, is your standup written for you from the board: what
you finished, what waits for review, what is blocked and what is moving, by organization. Its
count is the cards finished today.

## Reading it

Pick the span at the top: **Today**, **Since yesterday** (from yesterday's start) or **Last 7
days**. Under each organization:

| Part | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Done** | cards finished in the span, archived ones too, oldest first |
| **In review** | cards in Review that changed in the span |
| **Blocked** | cards in Blocked that changed in the span, with why |
| **In progress** | cards in progress that changed in the span (an autopilot's own cards stay under it) |

Each line is the card's key and title, its tickets and pull requests, and the time its Claude
worked on it. **Copy** puts the whole digest on the clipboard as Markdown, ready to paste into a
chat or a standup note.

To have it sent to your phone every morning, add a [trigger](/orgs/triggers/):

```toml
[[trigger]]
name = "standup"
on = "digest"
at = "09:15"
days = ["mon", "tue", "wed", "thu", "fri"]
```

## Work time

desk keeps how long each card's Claude worked: from each message (or a turn Claude starts on its
own) to the turn's end, added up. The digest shows it, and a Jira or Linear account can log it on
the ticket when the card goes to Done. Turn it on per account in [Settings](/orgs/settings/#git--review),
on the organization's **Git & review** page (**Work time on tickets**) or on the account's card, or
in its `desk.toml`:

```toml
[[jira]]
url = "https://globex.atlassian.net"
projects = ["ABC"]
worklog = true
```

| Tracker | What lands there |
|---|---|
| Jira | a worklog on the issue: the time in whole minutes (one at least), with the card's key and title as its comment |
| Linear | a comment, "Time spent: 1h 20m. DESK-7: …" (Linear has no worklog) |

Only time not logged yet goes, so a card that comes back from Done and goes there again logs the
new work alone. A toast says what was logged, or why not; a failure isn't retried for the same
work.
