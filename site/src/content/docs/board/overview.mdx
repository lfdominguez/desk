The board is desk's main window. Every card on it is one Claude Code session.
The sidebar is on the left, the toolbar runs along the top, and the columns
fill the rest.

![The desk board: the sidebar on the left, the toolbar on top, four columns of cards](../../../assets/shots/board-overview.png)

## Columns

A board has six columns: **Backlog**, **To do**, **In progress**, **Blocked**, **Review** and **Done**.
Each has its own color: To do is blue, In progress yellow, Blocked red, Review cyan, Done green.

The board never shows the **Done** column. Done cards wait in the
[Done archive](/board/archive/) instead, so the board holds only work that's still open.

Sessions move their own cards. Sending a message moves the card to In progress.
When Claude reports it's blocked, the card moves to Blocked. When Claude reports it's done, the card moves to
Review, once none of its subagents or background shells is still running. Nothing moves a card to Done but you.

![The To do column's header: its color dot, its name, a count of 2 and a + button](../../../assets/shots/board-overview-column.png)

<figcaption>A column's header shows how many cards it holds. While a search or a view hides some, it reads "shown/total", like 1/2.</figcaption>
</figure>

There are two ways to add a card to a column: the **+** in its header, or **Add card** at
its foot. Both open the [new card dialog](/cards/new-card/) for that column.

</figure>

## The toolbar

<figure class="cut">

![The toolbar: the sidebar toggle, the view's title and its summary, the archive box, the search field and New card](../../../assets/shots/board-overview-toolbar.png)

<figcaption>From left to right: the sidebar toggle, the view's name and its summary, then the archive box, the search field and New card.</figcaption>

- **Sidebar toggle**: shows or hides the sidebar.
- **The header**: the view's name, with its summary under it.
- **Unlock _vault_**: shows up only when a password vault is waiting for its password. See [Vaults](/orgs/vaults/).
- **The archive box**: archives every card in Done. See [Done archive](/board/archive/).
- **The search field**: filters the cards. See [Search](/board/search/).
- **New card** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>): opens the [new card dialog](/cards/new-card/).

### The header line

The header names the view you're in ("All cards", "Your turn", "#api", "Acme"…).
Under the name, a line sums up the view's cards by status, such as
"2 your turn · 1 blocked". It counts the cards that are working, waiting on your
turn, blocked or failed, and the ones past their due date ("1 overdue"). When
none of those apply, it just counts the cards ("3 cards"). Done cards don't count.

## Sidebar on and off

The toggle at the toolbar's far left shows or hides the [sidebar](/board/sidebar/).
With the sidebar hidden, the columns spread across the whole window.

## Moving cards

To move a card, drag it to another column, or higher or lower in the same one.
While you drag, a blue line shows where the card will land. Its old place stays
faded until you let go.

![A card dragged from To do into In progress: the card follows the pointer and a blue line marks where it lands](../../../assets/shots/board-drag.png)

<figure class="cut">

![Dark mode for the dashboard, dragged out of To do and over In progress, under the e2e card](../../../assets/shots/board-drag-columns.png)

<figcaption>Dragging DESK-4 from To do to In progress.</figcaption>
</figure>

A click on a card, without dragging it, opens its [session panel](/sessions/panel/).
You can also move a card from its [right-click menu](/board/cards/#the-card-menu) (**Move to**).

## Keys

Board keys work only on the board itself, not while you type in a field or a dialog.

| Do | Keys |
|---|---|
| Select a card | the arrow keys |
| Move the selected card | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+arrows: up or down in its column, or to the column beside it |
| Open its session | <kbd>Enter</kbd> |
| Edit it | <kbd>E</kbd> |
| Open its terminal | <kbd>T</kbd> |
| New card | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>, in the selected card's column (else Backlog) |
| Search | <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> |
| Go anywhere | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, the [palette](/board/palette/) |
| Back out | <kbd>Esc</kbd>: the dialog, the search, the panel, then the selection |

The full list is in [Keys](/reference/keys/).
