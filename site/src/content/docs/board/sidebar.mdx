title: The sidebar
description: Every view in the sidebar, what each one shows, and what its count counts.
---

The sidebar lists the board's views. Click one to show only its cards. The
toolbar's header then names the view and sums it up. Most rows end with a count.
A gray count of 0 means the view is empty right now.

![The sidebar beside the board: Board, Agent Sessions, Sessions, Organizations with Tickets, Alerts and CI under them, then Labels](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar.png)

The sidebar scrolls. The rest of it (labels, links, due dates and the archive) is further down:

![The sidebar scrolled to its end: Organizations, Labels, Links, Due and Archive](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-end.png)

:::note
Counts leave out cards in Done. Those are counted in **Done archive**.
:::

## Board: All cards

**All cards** is the whole board, every column but Done. Its count is the number
of cards on the board.

<figure class="cut">

![Board: All cards, 7](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-all.png)

<figcaption>All cards, the default view.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Agent Sessions

Every card with a session is listed here as its key and title, grouped by
organization when the sessions span more than one. Cards in Done drop off the
list: the Done archive has them.

<figure class="cut">

![Agent Sessions: under Acme DESK-6, under Globex DESK-5, under Initech DESK-7, each with its status dot](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-agents.png)

<figcaption>One row per session, under its organization.</figcaption>
</figure>

- The dot is the session's status color (blue for your turn, orange for blocked…).
- A green terminal icon means the card's [Claude Code terminal](/sessions/terminal/) is open.
- An orange warning icon means Claude is [asking you something](/sessions/questions/).

A click opens the session in the [full window](/sessions/full-window/), or
its terminal when one is open. These rows have no count.

## Sessions

The cards whose session is in a given state: **Working**, **Your turn**, **Blocked**
and **Failed**. Each count is the number of cards in that state.

<figure class="cut">

![Sessions: Working 0, Your turn 2, Blocked 1, Failed 0](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-status.png)

<figcaption>The sessions by status.</figcaption>
</figure>

**Your turn** is the one to check first: those sessions are waiting on you.

![The Your turn view: only the two cards waiting on you, the header reading "Your turn" over "2 your turn"](../../../assets/shots/board-view.png)

## Organizations

Each organization the board uses, by name, with a count of its cards. Click one
to show only its cards. A new card made from that view starts in that organization.

<figure class="cut">

![Organizations: Acme 2 with Tickets 3 and CI 1, Globex 3 with Tickets 0, Alerts 2 and CI 0, Initech 2 with Tickets 0](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-orgs.png)

<figcaption>Each organization, and its own views under it.</figcaption>
</figure>

An organization can have views of its own, under its name:

- **Tickets**: the open issues assigned to you in its Jira, GitHub or GitLab. The count is how many. See [My tickets](/orgs/tickets/).
- **Alerts**: its Nagios alerts. The count is the ones nobody acknowledged, in the worst one's color. See [Alerts](/orgs/alerts/).
- **CI**: the pipelines of its cards' pull requests, merge requests and commits. The count is the failures, in red. See [CI](/orgs/ci/).
- **Servers**: its servers, when it has a servers file. See [Servers](/servers/overview/).
- **Drawings**: its drawings. The count is how many. An organization with drawings shows here
  even without cards. See [Drawings](/drawings/overview/).

While desk is fetching an organization's links, tickets or alerts, a spinner takes the place of its dot.

The section's title has two buttons:

<figure class="cut">

![The Organizations title with Reload and Settings](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-reload.png)

<figcaption>Reload and Settings.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Reload** looks for organizations again (a folder you just made shows up), then rechecks every link, ticket, alert and CI run. A toast says how many organizations it found.
- **Settings** opens [the settings](/orgs/settings/) on the organization you're looking at, or the selected card's.

## Labels

Every label in use on the board, each in its own color (the same color as on the
cards), with the number of cards that carry it. The header names a label view
with a `#`, like "#api". A new card made from a label view starts with that label.

<figure class="cut">

![Labels: api, auth, billing, db, deps, infra, tests and web, each with a colored dot and a count](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-end-labels.png)

<figcaption>Labels in use, A to Z.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Links

Cards by the state of their links. See [Links](/cards/links/) for what the states mean.

<figure class="cut">

![Links: Waiting on review 0, Ready to merge 1, Changes requested · CI failed 1, Open tickets 1](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-end-links.png)

<figcaption>Four link views.</figcaption>
</figure>

| View | Its cards have |
|---|---|
| **Waiting on review** | a pull or merge request still open, waiting (gray) |
| **Ready to merge** | a pull or merge request approved (green) |
| **Changes requested · CI failed** | a pull or merge request with changes requested, or whose CI failed (red) |
| **Open tickets** | a Jira issue that isn't done |

## Due

<figure class="cut">

![Due: Overdue 0, This week 2](../../../assets/shots/board-sidebar-end-due.png)

<figcaption>Cards by due date.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Overdue**: cards whose due date has passed.
- **This week**: cards due today or in the next six days.

## Archive: Done archive

**Done archive** shows the Done column's cards, then the archived ones. Its count
is both together. See [Done archive](/board/archive/).
