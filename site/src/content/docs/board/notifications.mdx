title: Notifications
description: When desk tells your desktop that a card needs you, and when it stays quiet.
---

You don't have to keep an eye on the board. When a card's session turns to
something that needs you, desk shows a desktop notification.

## When one fires

A notification fires when a card's status changes to one of these:

| Status | The notification's title |
|---|---|
| Your turn | `DESK-5 · Your turn` |
| Blocked | `DESK-7 · Blocked` |
| Failed | `DESK-6 · Failed` |
| Ready for review | `DESK-8 · Ready for review` |

Its body is the card's title, then the reason Claude gave (or, without one, the start
of its last message) as plain text.

Those are the cards the sidebar's **Your turn**, **Blocked**, **Failed** and **Review** views
gather, so a notification's card is easy to find there:

## When it doesn't

- **You're already looking at the card.** desk's window has the keyboard and the card's
  session is open, in the panel or full window.
- **You stopped the session yourself.** A stop is your own doing, so there's nothing to tell you.
- **The status didn't change.** A card that's already your turn doesn't notify again.
- **The session is working.** Working never notifies: the session doesn't need you yet.

:::note
desk sends notifications through your desktop's notification service (freedesktop
notifications, over D-Bus). Under a compositor without a notification daemon
(mako, dunst, swaync, your desktop's own…) nothing shows.
:::
