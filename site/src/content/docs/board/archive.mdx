The board doesn't show the Done column. Cards you move to Done (from Review, once you've
checked Claude's work, or from any column) wait in the **Done archive**, along with the cards you archived.

Open it from the bottom of the sidebar: **Archive** → **Done archive**. Its count
is the Done cards plus the archived ones. The header reads "1 done · 1 archived".

![The Done archive: DESK-8 "Done · not archived yet" above DESK-6 "Archived from In progress"](../../../assets/shots/board-archive.png)

![Two cards in the archive: "Done … · not archived yet" over DESK-8, "Archived … from In progress" over DESK-6](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-list.png)

The toolbar's search filters the archive too. See [Search](/board/search/).

## Archive a card

Archiving takes a card off the board and into the archive, from any column. If
its session is running, desk stops it.

- Right-click the card and choose **Archive** (or press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> while its menu is open). See [the card menu](/board/cards/#the-card-menu).
- Or click the archive icon in the card's [session panel](/sessions/panel/).

A toast at the window's foot says what happened: "Archived DESK-6", or
"Archived DESK-6; its session stopped".

### Undo

<figure class="cut">

![The toast "Archived DESK-6" with an Undo button, its Ctrl+Z key and a bar for the time left](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-undo-toast.png)

<figcaption>The bar on the right runs down. Once it's gone, the undo is gone too.</figcaption>
</figure>

Click **Undo**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> on the board, within six seconds.
The card goes back to its column.

## Archive every Done card

The archive box in the toolbar archives every card in Done at once. A toast says
how many ("Archived 3 done cards"). This one has no undo, but you can still restore each card.

<figure class="cut">

![The toolbar's archive box, outlined, with its tooltip "Archive every card in Done"](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-all.png)

<figcaption>Archive every card in Done.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Restore a card

<Steps>

1. Open **Done archive** and click the card. Its session panel opens: you can read the conversation.
2. Click the archive icon in the panel's header, **Restore to the board**.

</Steps>

The card goes back to the end of the column it was archived from, and desk switches
back to All cards. A toast says "Restored DESK-6".

![An archived card's panel open beside the archive, its Restore to the board button outlined](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-restore.png)

<figure class="cut">

![The Restore to the board button in the panel's header](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-restore-restore.png)

<figcaption>Restore to the board.</figcaption>
</figure>

:::note
An archived card is read-only. You can't edit it or message its session until it's back on the board.
:::
