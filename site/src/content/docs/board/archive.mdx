The board doesn't show the Done column. Cards you move to Done (from Review, once you've
checked Claude's work, or from any column) wait in the **Done archive**, along with the cards you archived.

Open it from the bottom of the sidebar: **Archive** → **Done archive**. Its count
is the Done cards plus the archived ones. The header reads "1 done · 5 archived".

## Group and filter

**Group by** sorts the cards into sections, newest first:

- **Date**: Today, Yesterday, This week, This month, then one section per earlier month, by when each card went to Done.
- **Organization**: one section per organization, alphabetical, with cards that have none last.

The chips beside it filter the list. Each shows how many of the cards it would match.

- **Done** and **Archived** start lit. Click one to hide that kind.
- Each organization, and the six labels used most, start unlit. Light one to show only the cards
  that have it. Lit chips of the same kind add up: Acme and Globex together show both.

The toolbar's search filters the archive too. See [Search](/board/search/).

## Preview a card

Click a row to read the card beside the list, without leaving the archive. The preview shows:

- its key, title and organization, and when it finished
- **Open session**, **Restore** (an archived card) or **Archive** (a Done card), and **Delete**
- its labels, links, turns, tokens and cost
- its notes, and its last five messages, read-only

![An archived card previewed on the right of the archive: Open session, Restore and Delete over its labels, links and numbers](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-preview.png)

**Open session** shows the whole conversation full window. An archived card's session is
read-only until it's back on the board.

## Act on many cards

Each row has a checkbox, and each section's header has one for all its cards. Once a card is
checked, a bar over the list says how many and offers:

- **Archive**: the Done cards checked go to the archive (shown when one is checked).
- **Restore**: the archived cards checked go back to the board (shown when one is checked).
- **Delete**: the checked cards are deleted. Their conversation files stay on disk.
- **Clear**: uncheck everything.

![Two cards checked, one a whole section; the bar "2 selected" with Restore, Delete and Clear](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-bulk.png)

The bar acts only on checked cards the search and chips show. One toast undoes the whole
action: see [Undo](#undo).

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Preview the card above or below |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Check or uncheck the previewed card |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> | Check every card shown |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open the previewed card's session |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Delete the checked cards, or the previewed one when none are checked |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Uncheck everything, then clear the search |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo, while the toast shows |

## Archive a card

Archiving takes a card off the board and into the archive, from any column. If
its session is running, desk stops it.

- Right-click the card and choose **Archive** (or press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> while its menu is open). See [the card menu](/board/cards/#the-card-menu).
- Or click the archive icon in the card's [session panel](/sessions/panel/).
- Or, for Done cards, **Archive** in the archive's preview or its bar.

A toast at the window's foot says what happened: "Archived DESK-6", or
"Archived DESK-6; its session stopped".

### Undo

<figure class="cut">

![The toast "Archived DESK-6" with an Undo button, its Ctrl+Z key and a bar for the time left](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-undo-toast.png)

<figcaption>The bar on the right runs down. Once it's gone, the undo is gone too.</figcaption>
</figure>

Click **Undo**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>, within six seconds. The card goes back
where it was. After an action on many cards ("Archived 3 cards", "Deleted 2 cards"), every
card goes back where it was, as it was.

## Archive every Done card

The archive box in the toolbar archives every card in Done at once. A toast says
how many ("Archived 3 done cards"). This one has no undo, but you can still restore each card.

## Restore a card

<Steps>

1. Open **Done archive** and click the card.
2. Click **Restore** in the preview. To restore several, check them and click **Restore** in the bar.

</Steps>

The card goes back to the end of the column it was archived from. A toast says "Restored DESK-6".

<figure class="cut">

![The preview's Open session, Restore and Delete buttons, Restore outlined](../../../assets/shots/board-archive-preview-actions.png)

<figcaption>Restore, beside Open session and Delete.</figcaption>
</figure>

The archive icon in an archived card's [session panel](/sessions/panel/) restores it too.

:::note
An archived card is read-only. You can't edit it or message its session until it's back on the board.
:::
